01 / CONCEPT

VISION & MISSION

Cultural Storytelling Through Fashion

The UNIQUE Fashion Foundation envisions a global stage where fashion becomes a powerful medium for cultural storytelling. We aspire to lead the industry toward sustainability by supporting designers committed to eco-friendly practices.

By integrating cutting-edge technology, including a permanent presence in the Metaverse, we aim to revolutionize how fashion is showcased — creating immersive, inclusive, and innovative experiences that connect designers, buyers, and audiences worldwide.

04 / CURATED COLLABORATIONS

ART & FASHION EXHIBITIONS

TRANSCENDENTAL ART

LYNCH: TRANSITION TO THE TRANSCENDENTAL

ROMANOZ — PIONEER OF TRANSCENDENTAL ART

Artist Romanoz unveils a world beyond physical existence, presenting the transcendental dimension into which David Lynch and Björk have drawn inspiration. With over 28 years of continuous exploration, Romanoz is world-renowned for studying consciousness and visual perception.

✦ 2019 Stratosphere Project: World’s first art exhibition launched into the Stratosphere at 43 km altitude.
✦ Private Collections: Masterpieces held in permanent collections of David Lynch and Björk.
✦ Paris 2025 Premiere: Exclusive unveiling of the brand new metaphysical paintings and sculpture series.
DIGITAL FASHION & VR

THE SCARF AS A SYMBOL OF FREEDOM

CHANDRA LI (ELIZAVETA KHAUSTOVICH)

"A surreal, autobiographical, psychosomatic story of all women in my mind."

For centuries, the scarf has accompanied women, reflecting their identity, beliefs, and status. It could serve as a protective veil or a barrier that conceals.

✦ Physical & Digital Collection: Wearable silk haute couture alongside 3D VR digital wearable replicas.
✦ Surrealist Narrative: Exploring the fine line between female vulnerability, heritage, and unlimited freedom.
